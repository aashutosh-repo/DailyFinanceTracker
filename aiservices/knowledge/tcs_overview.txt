Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Tata Consultancy Services is an Indian multinational
information technology services and consulting company.

TCS provides services including consulting, application
development, cloud services, cybersecurity, data analytics,
artificial intelligence and business process services.

TCS operates across multiple industries including banking,
financial services, insurance, retail, manufacturing,
telecommunications and healthcare.

TCS is headquartered in Mumbai, India.

The company is part of the Tata Group.
