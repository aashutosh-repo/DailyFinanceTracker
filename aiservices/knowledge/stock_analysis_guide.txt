Stock Analysis Concepts

Price Change

Price change represents the difference between the ending
price and starting price during a selected period.

Percentage Price Change

Percentage price change is calculated as:

((Ending Price - Starting Price) / Starting Price) * 100

A positive percentage indicates an increase in price.
A negative percentage indicates a decrease.

Highest Price

The highest price represents the maximum trading high
observed during the selected period.

Lowest Price

The lowest price represents the minimum trading low
observed during the selected period.

Average Price

Average price can be calculated using the mean of the
closing prices during the selected period.

Historical performance does not guarantee future returns.
